Our Vision

A region where every learner is equipped with quality, relevant, and equitable education — produced through a responsive curriculum, excellent teaching, and exceptional educational leadership that transforms lives and communities.

Our Mission

To develop, implement, and manage responsive curricula and learning resources; provide technical assistance to schools; monitor and evaluate learning outcomes; and foster inclusive, learner-centered, and evidence-based education across all divisions of Region XII.
